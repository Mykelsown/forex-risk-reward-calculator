# 📊 Forex Risk-to-Reward Calculator

A simple, clean, and intuitive **Forex Risk-to-Reward Calculator** that helps traders plan trades by calculating risk, reward, position size, and risk-to-reward ratio before entering the market.

This project focuses on **proper risk management**, one of the most important principles in forex trading.

---

## 🚀 Live Demo
🔗 https://mykelsown.github.io/forex-risk-reward-calculator/

---

## 🧠 Features

- Calculate **risk amount** based on account balance and risk percentage
- Automatically determine **risk-to-reward ratio**
- Calculate **potential reward**
- Compute **position size**
- Supports both **buy and sell** trade scenarios
- Clean, responsive, and user-friendly interface
- Built with a modern utility-first CSS approach

---

## 📝 How It Works

1. Enter your **Account Balance**
2. Input your **Risk Percentage (%)**
3. Enter your **Entry Price**
4. Enter your **Stop Loss Price**
5. Enter your **Take Profit Price**
6. Click **Calculate**
7. Instantly view:
   - Risk Amount
   - Reward Amount
   - Risk-to-Reward Ratio
   - Position Size

---

## 🧮 Calculation Logic

- **Risk Amount**

Account Balance × (Risk % ÷ 100)


- **Stop Loss Distance**


|Entry Price − Stop Loss Price|


- **Take Profit Distance**


|Take Profit Price − Entry Price|


- **Risk-to-Reward Ratio**


Take Profit Distance ÷ Stop Loss Distance


- **Position Size**


Risk Amount ÷ Stop Loss Distance
